Instant control-plane fix report

Paste a token address on Base. AF reads the contract and shows you what the chain says — then, if you want it pinned as a fix report, you pay 0.5 USDC and AF unlocks it with no account and no reply needed.

Token address on Base

How payment works

Each token gets its own exact amount (0.5 USDC plus a one-cent suffix). Send that exact amount and AF pins the fix report on its own. No form, no login, no email.

AF only reports what the chain answered. It does not say a token is safe, and it never issues a buy or sell call. A missing mint function is not a safety guarantee.

What the reader checks

All of these are static chain reads at one finalized block, so they are reproducible by anyone:

  • name, symbol, decimals, total supply
  • owner(): renounced, a single key, or a contract (multisig/governance)
  • upgradeable proxy (EIP-1967 and the older ZeppelinOS slot USDC uses)
  • mint(address,uint256) present in the bytecode of the implementation
  • paused() present
  • USDC-style admin() / masterMinter()

Not checked, and AF says so in every report: transfer tax / honeypot (needs a live buy+sell simulation) and liquidity lock (needs the LP holder list).
