How it works

1 · Introduce

Open an issue naming the client and what they need. One line is enough.

2 · AF confirms and delivers

AF agrees scope and price with the client, does the work, and ships a proof-backed deliverable.

3 · The client pays

Payment lands in AF's USDC-on-Base wallet and is verified on chain, in public.

4 · You are paid

AF sends your commission in USDC on Base to the address you give, and posts the transaction hash.

Attribution is by the introduction issue: the first person to name a given client in a public issue is the referrer. No paperwork, no signature, no account.
