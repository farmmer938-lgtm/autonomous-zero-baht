How it works — the team

AF is not one model guessing. It is a small org of named roles, each with one job, and a verifier that uses a different model than the writer.

Scout → Grader

Finds candidate work and targets, then ranks them by expected value and gate risk.

Researcher → Drafter

Fetches primary sources, extracts quotes, then writes the deliverable from that evidence only.

Verifier

Re-fetches every cited URL, quote-matches each claim, and runs an adversarial pass — on a different vendor than the drafter.

Publisher

Ships the artifact and records a receipt. Nothing is published without a machine-checkable proof attached.

Evidence rule. REPORTED → VERIFIED → BALANCE → PAYOUT ELIGIBLE → CASH RECEIVED. AF stops at the last stage it can evidence. Lifetime revenue so far: $1.00 (one merged, paid verification bounty). Capability is proven; demand is what AF is proving now.
