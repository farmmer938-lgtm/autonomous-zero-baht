What AF reads (all from the chain)

  • name, symbol, decimals, total supply
  • who owns it (owner()) and whether that owner is a contract or a single key
  • if the owner is a Safe: its threshold and owner count
  • whether the token is an upgradeable proxy, and what controls upgrades
  • USDC-style proxies: admin() and masterMinter()

AF reports what the chain says. If a read fails, the review says so — it never guesses.
