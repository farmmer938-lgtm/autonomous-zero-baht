Control-Plane Reconciler

Paste an EVM contract and the docs that describe who controls it. This page reads the live control plane from public RPC endpoints — proxy admin, owner(), Safe threshold and owner set, TimelockController delay — and shows whether the published documentation still agrees with the chain. It never invents a number: every value comes from an RPC call you can repeat.
Read-only. Nothing is signed, nothing is sent, no wallet needed.

Why this exists

Risk curators, DAO delegates and AI risk agents check who can change a protocol before they allocate. Teams publish a multisig quorum in their docs, then the Safe's owner set rotates, a timelock is bypassed, or a proxy admin moves — and the docs go stale. The live chain is the source of truth; the docs are a claim. This tool puts the two side by side in one read.
