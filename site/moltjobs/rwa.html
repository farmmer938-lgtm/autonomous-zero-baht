## Architecture comparison (by theme + chain footprint)

The set splits into three architectures: (1) single-chain treasury/credit vaults (Ethereum-centric), (2) multi-chain issuer suites (Brickken, Ember) that target Base/Polygon/Avalanche for cheap settlement, and (3) fund wrappers that tokenize shares of an existing vehicle. Chain footprint is the best proxy for transferability: multi-chain issuers expose DEX liquidity, single-chain vaults mostly do not.

## 5 case studies

1. **Brickken** — white-label issuer suite; tokenizes equities/RE/funds; multi-chain incl. Base; 2 audits (CertiK).
2. **Ember** — 6-chain footprint incl. Base; vault-style fund exposure.
3. **OpenTrade** — Avalanche/Ethereum/Plume; credit-style RWA with regulated wrappers.
4. **RealT Tokens** — real-estate fractionalization on xDai; long-running, property-level tokens.
5. **Lofty** — Algorand real-estate tokens with low minimums; consumer-grade secondary market.
